# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary users are couples, families, private hosts, and international clients planning weddings, birthdays, dinners, and private events in Marrakech or Morocco. Many arrive from Instagram on mobile and need to feel, quickly, that Green Events Maroc can create a beautiful, organized, premium celebration.

Secondary users include guests, collaborators, and venue partners who may use the site to understand the brand's taste level, service scope, and visual standard.

## Product Purpose

Green Events Maroc is a production client website for a Marrakech-based wedding, catering, and private events brand. The site exists to sell the experience of a tailor-made celebration where catering, decoration, styling, atmosphere, and coordination feel unified.

Success means visitors feel trust, romance, warmth, and precision, then take one of the intended actions: request a WhatsApp quote, view the portfolio, visit Instagram, or call directly.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, warm, precise.

The voice is elegant, human, calm, and premium. It should feel like a luxury wedding invitation and an event portfolio, with Moroccan warmth carried through imagery, light, texture, service rhythm, and refined details.

The default language is French, with English available through a clean language switcher. Visible content must use French and English only.

## Anti-references

The site must not look like:

- A generic SaaS landing page or dashboard.
- A cheap card grid template.
- An AI-generated wedding site with vague luxury language.
- A neon, startup-gradient, or overdecorated event page.
- A stock-photo brochure with disconnected sections.
- A corporate catering site that lists services without selling atmosphere.
- A motion demo where animation distracts from readability or conversion.

Avoid long text blocks, random icons, bouncy motion, heavy dark overlays that kill photography, repeated generic section scaffolding, and Arabic visible content.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with atmosphere: imagery, light, table details, flowers, candles, food, and warm evening scenes should carry the first emotional impression.
2. Sell one unified experience: decoration, catering, styling, coordination, and hospitality should feel composed as one event, not separate services.
3. Treat mobile as the primary venue: Instagram visitors on phones must get the richest, clearest version of the brand, not a reduced desktop fallback.
4. Use motion only when it deepens the story: scroll effects should feel calm, premium, and purposeful, with reduced-motion fallbacks that keep all content visible.
5. Keep conversion discreet and clear: WhatsApp quote requests should be easy to reach without turning the page into a loud sales funnel.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for body text and controls. Keep text readable on small screens, avoid horizontal overflow, provide meaningful alt text for imagery, and make buttons and links accessible by keyboard and screen reader.

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all animation. The site must remain usable and complete without pinned or scrubbed scroll effects. The language system must keep visible content in one language per content block.
